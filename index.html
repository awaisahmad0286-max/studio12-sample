A Soho institution

Tucked among the bustle of Dean Street, Studio 12 is an industrial-chic salon of bare brick and brushed steel — and some of the most experienced hands in London hairdressing. Every stylist here has at least 25 years' experience, and each has their own speciality; tell us what your hair needs and we'll match you with the right person.

No pressure, no hard sell on products you don't need — just honest advice, great conversation, and hair you'll love. Two minutes from Tottenham Court Road station.
